## ⚠️ Incident Report: Informatics Class

### What was agreed:
In the morning meeting, **Kyrian Weiss** and **Ivan Weiss** (co-founders) agreed that **Jonathan Verdun** (CTO) would **not** be the one teaching the Informatics class.

### What happened:
When the class started, Jonathan was the one explaining everything.

### Root cause:
Ivan had separately agreed with Jonathan to let him lead the class as a learning opportunity (public speaking practice).

### Issues identified:

1. **Professionalism concern:**
   - Even though the course was offered for free, it's a professional engagement
   - A paid/free course is not the place for practice — it's where we demonstrate our capabilities
   - Quote: *"You don't treat a job like practice"*

2. **Communication breakdown:**
   - Decisions made in meetings were changed without re-discussing with all parties
   - Side agreements were made that contradicted the group decision

### Lessons learned:
- ✅ It's not wrong that Jonathan wants to learn public speaking
- ❌ It wasn't the right time or venue
- ❌ Changes to agreed decisions need to be discussed before implementation

---

## 📋 Action Items Resulting from This

### New Meeting Protocol Established

> **"From now on we will be having meetings as often as needed to keep communication channels open."**

See `meeting-protocols.md` for the full structure.

## 🎯 Key Takeaways for the Team

1. Meetings are where decisions are made
2. If something needs to change after a meeting, schedule another meeting
3. The Drive folder with transcriptions is our **source of truth**
4. Professional standards apply even to free/volunteer work
