## 📋 2026 Business Rules & Advice

### 🔴 The Golden Rules

1. **"If it's not written, it doesn't exist"**
   - Never improvise agreements
   - All roles in writing
   - Everyone signs (written commitment connects to brain)

2. **Avoid the phrase: "We'll see later"**
   - This is the worst phrase for business
   - Decisions made = decisions documented

3. **Choose ONE communication channel**
   - Don't let Ivan change it constantly
   - Consistency is key

### 📝 Define Roles Clearly (In Writing)

| Role Question | Must Be Defined |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Who decides? | ✅ |
| Who executes? | ✅ |
| Who communicates? | ✅ |
| Who has final decision? | ✅ |

### 💰 Financial Structure (Do NOW, Before Money Comes)

- Define percentages NOW (before deals close)
- Example structure:
  - Kyrian & Ivan: percentage of earnings
  - Jonathan: hourly rate (market value)
  - Company account: portion of all earnings
- **Must be written and signed**
- Never associate without contracts

### 🗣️ Communication Strategy with Ivan

**Understanding the difference:**
- Ivan (Gemini): Fast mental rhythm, changes ideas quickly, needs to express thoughts verbally
- Kyrian (Pisces): Thinks in images, intuitive, needs structure

**Rules:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Talk from the ROLE | Talk from emotion |
| Speak calmly | Raise voice (he closes down) |
| Be two steps ahead | React impulsively |
| Bring irrefutable evidence | Bring opinions |
| Analyze before meetings | Be surprised |
| Say "Can we talk?" | Use complaining tone |

**Key insight:** When you raise your voice with Ivan, it triggers the same response as his mom complaining about him. He won't listen.
