# 📅 Ai-Whisperers Meeting Protocols

**Effective Date:** February 9, 2026  
**Established by:** Kyrian Weiss

---

## 🎯 Purpose

To maintain open communication channels and ensure all team decisions are:
- Properly discussed
- Documented
- Followed through

---

## 📆 Regular Meeting Schedule

### Monday Meeting — "Week Kickoff"
**Purpose:**
- Discuss what we're working on this week
- Communicate tasks to team members
- Present upcoming projects
- Align priorities

### Friday Meeting — "Week Wrap-up"  
**Purpose:**
- Review progress on weekly tasks
- Discuss blockers and how to resolve them
- Plan completion of remaining items
- Celebrate wins

### Ad-hoc Meetings
- Scheduled as needed during the week
- **Required** when changing something that was previously agreed upon

---

## 🗂️ Documentation Process

### After Each Meeting:

1. **Physical notes:** Kyrian maintains handwritten notes in personal book

2. **Digital transcription:** 
   - Use the transcription tool: [github.com/Ai-Whisperers/transcriptions](https://github.com/Ai-Whisperers/transcriptions)
   - Analyze and summarize key points
   - Store on team Drive

3. **Source of truth:** 
   - [Google Drive - Meeting Notes Folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1d6vwcPeR8jv8Sq7JOGM1IZDXYkT83Z7z)
   - This is the official record

## 📜 Decision-Making Rules

### Rule 1: Meetings = Decisions
What's agreed in a meeting is the decision.

### Rule 2: Changes Require Discussion
If something needs to change after a meeting:
- Schedule a new meeting
- Don't make side agreements that contradict group decisions

### Rule 3: Documentation
All important decisions must be in the Drive folder.

---

## 🎨 Personal Project Day

**One day per week:** Each team member can choose what project to work on.

**Rest of the week:** Focus on company goals and assigned tasks.
